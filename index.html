Privacy Policy

Malibu Cruiser • Last Updated: October 2026
Summary: Malibu Cruiser is built with an offline-first philosophy. We do not collect, harvest, store, or sell your personal data. All gameplay progress, high scores, garage unlocks, and custom license plates are stored locally on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of Fifth Raven Studios ("we", "our", or "us") on the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you play Malibu Cruiser (the "Game") on Android devices.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your right to privacy. We do not require account creation, login, or registration. Specifically, we do NOT collect, harvest, or store:

  • Your real name, age, gender, or personal profile data.
  • Your email address, phone number, or physical contact address.
  • Your device contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data.
  • Your precise GPS location.

3. Local Storage of Gameplay Data

All game data—including your meters driven, highest speed, overtakes, earned Cruiser Coins, account rank XP, unlocked cars, driver choices, custom paint finishes, and custom 3D license plate text—is stored locally and exclusively in your device's private application storage (using standard Android Preferences/SharedPreferences). This data remains on your physical device unless you choose to uninstall the application or wipe application data in Android Settings.

4. Third-Party Platform Services

When your mobile device is connected to the Internet, Malibu Cruiser may interface with standard, certified Google Play platform services:

  • Google Play Games Services: Provides optional cloud saves, achievements, and global high-score leaderboards. Google processes your gamer profile and submitted scores in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: If you choose to purchase optional digital items (such as the Starter Cruiser, VIP All-Access Pass, or Sunset Founder Edition), transactions are handled directly by Google Play. Fifth Raven Studios never receives, processes, or stores your credit card details, bank information, or billing address.
  • Google AdMob (Optional Rewarded Video Ads): For players playing the free version, you may optionally choose to watch a sponsored video to continue a run after a collision. Google AdMob may collect and process anonymous device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID) and device diagnostics to serve and measure non-personalized or personalized ads in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies. Players who own the VIP Pass, Founder Edition, or No-Ads tier experience zero advertising.

5. GDPR and International Player Data Rights

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable global privacy regulations, you hold the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Deletion: You can completely erase all local gameplay records at any time simply by clearing application storage in Android Settings, using the in-game reset option, or uninstalling the Game.
  • Right to Inquire: If you have questions regarding any Google Play Games Services records or wish to request assistance, you may contact our team at the email addresses provided below.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Malibu Cruiser is a non-violent driving game designed for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date and within the in-game document viewer.
